Identity

          

            A name and an MMSI are all that is needed. AIS broadcasts on MMSI and
            never on IMO, and there is no free way to look one up from the other, so
            it has to come off her radio licence or her AIS unit — but everything
            beside it here she broadcasts herself, and it is taken the first time she
            is heard. Fill any of it in and it is checked against what she says
            rather than replaced, which is what catches a wrong MMSI.
