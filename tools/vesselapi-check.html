VesselAPI check

Paste the key from the VesselAPI dashboard and press Ask. This makes one request for all sixty-one yachts and reports what came back. It costs one call off the allowance.

Their answer is a stream of position reports, newest first, twenty to a page — not one row per yacht. So one page shows you the shape of the data and proves the key works; it cannot tell you how many of the sixty-one they carry, because most of the fleet simply has not spoken in the last four minutes. For that, follow the cursor: node tools/fetch-vesselapi.js YOUR_KEY --pages 10.

The key is used for this one request and nothing else. It is not saved, not stored in the browser, and disappears when you close the tab — unlike the AIS key, which the board keeps on purpose.
